About us

Oslo, the way locals live it

We're a small local company for travellers who are tired of tourist attractions — and who'd rather spend a few hours living like someone who actually lives here.

Home › About us

Most visitors see Oslo through a coach window or a queue: the Opera House, the palace, a museum, a souvenir shop. They're fine. But they're not what makes people who live here love this city.

What we love is simpler. Taking the metro to the end of the line and walking into the forest. A filter coffee in a small café where the owner roasted the beans. A fire in the snow on a dark winter evening. That's the Oslo we want to share — and it's why we started Oslo Hiking Tours.

Who our walks are for

For travellers who've done the sights, or never wanted to. For people who'd rather sit in a neighbourhood café than a tourist restaurant, and who want to come home having experienced a place, not just photographed it.

What we believe in

  • Always local. We stop at local, independent cafés — never chains — and choose smaller, grassroots places over the big names.
  • Made here. Where there's food or drink on a walk, we go for things that are made locally.
  • Not a sales pitch. We keep things non-commercial. A walk with us is about the place and the people you're with — not about selling you something along the way.
  • Always private. Every tour is just your group and your guide. No strangers, no crowd — so everyone can relax and be themselves.

How we work

We're deliberately small. There's no ticket office and no fleet of buses — we meet you in the city and walk, or take the metro together like everyone else who lives here. Keeping things simple means we can stay flexible: your pace, your date, your questions.

Our walks today: the Oslo Coffee Walk through the city's best independent cafés, the Winter Forest Hike in Nordmarka, and the Campfire Evening Walk. Compare all our walking tours →

Part of a small family of local companies

Oslo Hiking Tours has two sister companies: Oslo Bike Tours, which shows visitors Oslo by bike, and Senja Fjord Tours in Northern Norway. Same idea everywhere: private, local, and honest.
