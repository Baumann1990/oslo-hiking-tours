Campfire Evening Walk

Winter afternoons in Oslo are short — the sun sets before four. But Norwegians don't stay in: they head into the forest with a headlamp and light a fire. This walk lets you do the same.

You meet your guide in central Oslo as the light fades and ride the metro up to Nordmarka. With headlamps on, you walk into the dark, snowy forest — quiet, still, and only minutes from the city. At a fire place in the woods, your guide lights a campfire, and you sit by the flames before walking back to the metro.

It's an easy, gentle evening — and a very Norwegian way to spend a winter night.

Highlights

  • Walk through the snowy forest by headlamp — Nordmarka at night
  • A campfire in the woods, lit by your guide
  • City lights below on clear evenings
  • Fits after a day of sightseeing — no early start
  • Private guide for your group only

Snow, ice or slush?

The walk runs whether the forest is snowy, icy or bare. When the trails are icy, your guide brings ice spikes and poles for everyone.

If the weather makes the forest unsafe, we'll tell you the evening before and offer a new date or a full refund.

What's included

  • Private guide for your group
  • Headlamps
  • Ice spikes and poles when the trails are icy
  • The campfire

What to bring

  • Warm, waterproof winter boots
  • Warm layers, a windproof jacket, hat and gloves — it gets cold sitting still
  • Your own snacks and a thermos, if you like — there's time to toast something over the fire
  • A metro ticket — a standard Ruter ticket for zone 1; we'll show you how to buy it in the app

Good to know

  • Starts around 16:00, after dark
  • About 3 hours in total, including roughly 30 minutes on the metro each way
  • Easy walking on forest trails
  • Children are welcome — contact us with ages
  • We send the exact meeting point in your confirmation

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to walk in the forest after dark?

Yes. Your guide knows the trails well, everyone has a headlamp, and the routes are easy forest paths close to the metro line.

Is food included?

No. Bring your own snacks and a thermos if you like — there's time to toast something over the fire.

Will we see the northern lights?

Probably not — the northern lights are rarely visible from Oslo. On clear nights you'll see stars and the city lights below, but we don't promise auroras.

How do we get to the forest?

By metro. You meet your guide in central Oslo and ride the metro together up to Nordmarka — about 30 minutes each way. You'll need a standard Ruter ticket for zone 1, which we'll help you buy in the app.

What should I wear?

Warm, waterproof winter boots, warm layers, a windproof jacket, hat and gloves. You'll be sitting still by the fire for a while, so dress warmer than for a walk. We send a full list with your confirmation.

When does the season run?

From December to March. Book by enquiry and we'll confirm your date within 24 hours.
