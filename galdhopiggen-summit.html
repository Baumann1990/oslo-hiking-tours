Galdhøpiggen Summit

Galdhøpiggen stands at 2,469 metres — the highest point in Norway, in Scandinavia and in all of Northern Europe. This is the flagship trip: a full day out of Oslo to the roof of the continent and back, with your private guide leading you all the way to the summit.

We pick you up at 05:00 and drive five hours northwest to Juvasshytta (1,841m), Norway's highest mountain hotel, accessible by road. From there the summer route climbs through a dramatic high-alpine landscape of rock, scree and sweeping ridgelines to the broad summit at 2,469m. On a clear day the view encompasses an enormous sweep of Norway — lakes, ridgelines and valleys in every direction, with nothing higher anywhere in the country.

We descend the same way, with time for a warm meal at Juvasshytta before the drive home. We're back in Oslo around midnight. It's a long day — and an unforgettable one.

Highlights

  • The highest peak in Norway and Scandinavia — 2,469m above sea level
  • Dramatic high-alpine terrain — rock, scree and open ridgelines
  • Juvasshytta base (1,841m) — Norway's highest car-accessible point
  • Summit panorama over Jotunheimen's ridges, lakes and valleys
  • One of the world's most accessible high-alpine summits

Good to know

  • Pickup at 05:00 — return to Oslo around midnight (a full 19-hour day)
  • Good fitness and some mountain experience recommended
  • Warm layers, waterproofs and sturdy hiking boots with ankle support are essential
  • Weather-dependent — your guide will advise if conditions require rescheduling
  • The summit is above 2,400m — altitude awareness recommended for sensitive individuals
