FAQ

Common questions

What winter tours do you offer?

Two private outings in Nordmarka, the forest on Oslo's doorstep: the Winter Forest Hike (about 4 hours, NOK 4,000 per group) and the Campfire Evening Walk (about 3 hours, NOK 3,500 per group). Both run from December to March.

Do I need any experience?

No. Both tours are easy walks — no skiing and no previous experience needed. Snowshoeing is simply walking with wider feet, and your guide shows you how.

What if there is no snow?

Snow in Oslo comes and goes, so our tours run in all winter conditions — with snowshoes when there's snow, and with ice spikes and poles when the trails are icy. If the weather makes the forest unsafe, we offer a new date or a full refund.

How do we get to the forest?

You meet your guide in central Oslo and ride the metro together up to Nordmarka — about 30 minutes each way. You'll need a standard Ruter ticket for zone 1, which we'll help you buy in the app.

Are the tours private?

Yes. Every tour is just your group and your guide — no strangers. Prices are per group of up to 4; larger groups are welcome, just ask for a quote.

What is included?

A private guide, plus snowshoes or ice spikes, poles and headlamps as needed. Metro tickets and food are not included — bring your own snack and a warm drink if you like.

What should I wear?

Warm, waterproof winter boots are the most important thing — the snowshoes and spikes go over your own boots. Add warm layers, a windproof jacket, a hat and gloves. We send a full list with your confirmation.

Do you run tours in summer?

Yes — in summer we run guided hikes and day trips from Oslo. The summer programme for 2027 will be published in spring.
