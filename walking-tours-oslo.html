Why a private walking tour?

Oslo has plenty of walking tours — most of them big groups following the same route past the Opera House, the Royal Palace and the City Hall. They're a good introduction. Ours are something different: just your group and a local guide, at your pace, on the day and time that suits you.

  • No strangers. Every tour is private — a couple, a family or a few friends, never a crowd.
  • The Oslo locals use. Coffee bars in St. Hanshaugen and Grünerløkka, the river path along the Akerselva, and Nordmarka, the forest that starts where the metro ends.
  • Easy walking. No experience or special fitness needed. We bring snowshoes, ice spikes and headlamps when the walk needs them.
  • Honest prices. A fixed price per group of up to 4, published on every tour page. Larger groups get a quote.

Which walk is right for you?

  • Love coffee, or want a city walk with warm stops? The Oslo Coffee Walk: four of Oslo's best specialty cafés, from the centre to Grünerløkka. About 3 hours, all year.
  • Want snow and forest? The Winter Forest Hike: by metro to Nordmarka, on snowshoes or ice spikes. About 4 hours, December to March.
  • Looking for something to do after dark? The Campfire Evening Walk: by headlamp into the forest to a campfire. About 3 hours, December to March.
  • Something else in mind? A longer walk, a different time or a special occasion — tell us what you're picturing.

Frequently asked questions

Are there private walking tours in Oslo?

Yes. All our walks are private — just your group and your guide, never strangers. Choose between the Oslo Coffee Walk in the city, the Winter Forest Hike in Nordmarka and the Campfire Evening Walk. Prices are per group of up to 4; larger groups can ask for a quote.

What is the best walking tour in Oslo in winter?

For a city walk with warm stops, the Oslo Coffee Walk (about 3 hours). For snow and forest, the Winter Forest Hike in Nordmarka (about 4 hours, snowshoes or ice spikes provided). For something very Norwegian after dark, the Campfire Evening Walk (about 3 hours).

How is a private walking tour different from a free walking tour?

A free walking tour is a large group following a set route in the city centre, with a tip at the end. A private tour is only your group, at your pace, on your date and time — and ours go beyond the usual sights, into Oslo's coffee neighbourhoods and the forest above the city.

How long are the walks and how far do we walk?

The coffee walk is about 3 hours and 4 km, with long café stops. The Winter Forest Hike is about 4 hours including the metro, and the Campfire Evening Walk about 3 hours. All are easy, and no experience is needed.

Where do the walking tours start?

All three start in central Oslo. The coffee walk ends in Grünerløkka; for the forest walks we ride the metro up to Nordmarka together. We send the exact meeting point in your confirmation.

How much does a private walking tour in Oslo cost?

NOK 3 000 per group for the Oslo Coffee Walk (coffee not included), NOK 3 500 for the Campfire Evening Walk and NOK 4 000 for the Winter Forest Hike — all for groups of up to 4.
